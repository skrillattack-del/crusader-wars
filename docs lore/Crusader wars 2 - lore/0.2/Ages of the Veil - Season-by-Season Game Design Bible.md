# AGES OF THE VEIL
## A Season-by-Season Game Design Bible for the Connected Universe
**Demon Slayer → Castlevania → Castlevania: Nocturne → Avatar: The Last Airbender → The Legend of Korra → Naruto / Shippuden → Hunter x Hunter → Jujutsu Kaisen**

Compiled October 2026 · Version 1.0

---

# HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

Everything below treats the eight franchises as sequential **Cycles** of one world. A few ground rules so the mapping stays consistent:

1. **Real-world dates are local calendars, not contradictions.** Demon Slayer plays in Taishō-era Japan, Castlevania in 1476 Wallachia, Nocturne in 1792 France — yet Demon Slayer is the *first* age. The fix is built into the lore: the **Infinite Corridor** (Castlevania S3) folds time. Each Cycle overlays the same territories after a world-reset called a **Withdrawal**. When a Cycle ends, the Veil closes, records burn, and the next bloom starts from surviving bloodlines and buried relics. Dates inside a Cycle use that Cycle's own calendar; Cycles are ordered by lore, not by clock.

2. **Every entry is a campaign node.** Each season, book, arc, or film gets: an in-fiction campaign name, an Age/local date, a protagonist, an antagonist, the party/main cast, the tools and tech-tree unlocks it grants, and a **Veil-hook** tying it to the wider universe.

3. **Tech tree has four branches, four tiers.** Every Age develops along:
   - **Discipline** — the era's energy system (breathing → oath-consecration → bloodline channeling → bending → chakra → nen → cursed energy)
   - **Arms** — weapons and carried tools
   - **Craft** — industry, medicine, forging, logistics
   - **Veilcraft** — spirit-world technology (portals, domains, summons, seals)

4. **Movies are canon unless flagged.** Canon films sit in the main timeline. Non-canon films sit in a **Corridor Pocket** slot — in-fiction they are *real events that happened in an adjacent timeline*, accessible through the Infinite Corridor, which is why they can contradict (this is the elegant trick that lets players visit them without breaking lore).

# PART I — THE COSMOLOGY IN FIVE LAWS

**Law 1: The Veil is one continuum with a spectrum.** Hell, the Speakers' "other side," the Demon Slayer dream realm, and the Avatar spirit world are the same place at different altitudes. Top layer: ancestor spirits and light (Raava's domain). Middle: wanderers — Koh, Wan Shi Tong, Hei Bai. Bottom: chaos and death, where Vaatu's residue pools and where Muzan's blood-will eventually sinks.

**Law 2: Corruption sinks, gifts rise.** Vampires, night creatures, demons, and curses are spirit-force dragged *down* the spectrum. Bending, nen, and sacred consecration are spirit-force flowing *up*. The Belmonts fought in the lower layers their whole lives without knowing the upper ones existed. The Avatar is the first being licensed to patrol the whole spectrum — that is what the Avatar State *is*.

**Law 3: The Vow is the world's currency.** Oaths are contracts with the spirit world. The slayer corps' vows, the Belmont whip's consecration, Fire Nation regalia, Uzumaki seals, Kurapika's Judgment Chain, and Jujutsu binding vows all run on one economy: sacrifice something, gain power. This setting's magic system, in game terms, is a vow-economy with compounding interest and brutal foreclosure terms.

**Law 4: The Vessel Cycle.** Certain mortals try to hold god-power. The attempts recur forever, and they form the spine of the whole timeline:
- **Yoriichi** (Demon Slayer) — the natural vessel, a man whose body held the sun; never sought godhood.
- **Erzsebet Báthory** (Nocturne) — consumes Sekhmet; a *meal*, not a bond. Fails.
- **The Rebis** (Castlevania S4) — alchemists weld opposites by force. Fails.
- **Wan and Raava** (ATLA, flashback era) — consent and fusion. *Succeeds.* The first Avatar.
- **Aang and Ozai's comet; Korra and Raava's loss and rebirth** — the doctrine tested.
- **Pain / Nagato** (Naruto) — god-vessel by stolen eyes. Fails, redeemed.
- **Kaguya** (Naruto) — vessel success without bond. Monstrous.
- **Riko Amanai / Star Plasma Vessel** (JJK) — the Cycle's cruelest recurrence; Geto's breaking point.
- **The Merger** (JJK, endgame) — Kenjaku's attempt to finish what the Rebis started.

**Law 5: Withdrawal follows every feast.** When a Cycle's war ends, the spirit world retreats — burned by human purges (witch trials), exhausted by war, or sealed by treaty. Magic goes latent, disciplines degrade into folklore, and the next Cycle begins with humanity re-learning what its ancestors knew. This is why the tech tree never climbs smoothly: every age re-invents the wheel after the flood.

# PART III — THE CAMPAIGNS, SEASON BY SEASON

# CYCLE 0 — THE AGE OF THE FIRST BREATH (Demon Slayer)

**Age overview.** Humanity's first systematic energy discipline: breathing, the forced internal circulation of chi to burn out corruption. The world's ambient spirit energy is still high from the last Withdrawal, which is why one man's bad medicine (Muzan) can spawn an entire demon ecology. Taishō industrialization is rising — this is the only Cycle where magic and modern tech briefly overlap.

**Age tech tree:**
- **Discipline:** T1 Total Concentration Breathing → T2 Constant / Flame of Life variants → T3 Breathing hybridization (self-taught forms) → T4 Transparent World & Selfless State
- **Arms:** T1 Nichirin ore blades (sun-eating metal) → T2 color-changing & crimson-forged blades → T3 mark-bearing weapons → T4 the Bright Red Blade
- **Craft:** T1 wisteria pharm → T2 demon-biology poisons (Tamayo) → T3 Kasugai crow logistics → T4 anti-Kibutsuji medicine
- **Veilcraft:** T1 dream-entry (blood demon art) → T2 demon pocket-spaces → T3 the Biwa-castle (Infinity Castle) → T4 the demon lord's shared blood-will

---

### Campaign 0.1 — "Ashes of the Kamado" (Season 1: Unwavering Resolve Arc, 2019)

- **Local date:** Taishō, winter. Mount Kumotori, Asakusa, Mount Fujikasane, Natagumo.
- **Protagonist:** Tanjiro Kamado — charcoal-seller turned slayer, bearer of the black blade.
- **Antagonist:** Muzan Kibutsuji, the First Demon (shadow antagonist); Lower Rank demons Rui and Enmu's network; Susamaru/Yahaba as mid-bosses.
- **Party / main cast:** Nezuko Kamado (demon-bound sister), Zenitsu Agatsuma, Inosuke Hashibira, Giyu Tomioka, Sakonji Urokodaki, Tamayo & Yushiro (rogue demon scientists).
- **Tools & unlocks:** Water Breathing (T1 discipline), first glimpse of Sun Breathing/Hinokami Kagura (T4 seed), Nichirin blade — black, "the blade that drinks no color" (T1 arms), Kasugai crow network (T3 craft), wisteria ward-lines (T1 craft).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks the **Slayer Corps** as a playable faction: final-selection survival mechanic, rank ladder (Mizunoto → Kinoe), breathing-slot system.
- **Veil-hook:** Tanjiro's black blade is forged of the same **sun-metal** the later Belmonts will beat into the Vampire Killer's chain. The ore remembers. And Muzan — a man remade by medicine who feared death — is the first human to *will himself* into the lower spectrum. His blood doesn't die when he does; it sinks, and it waits. Every curse, every night creature, every tailed beast in later ages is his blood-will's echo surfacing.

### Campaign 0.2 — "The Forty-Passenger Rite" (Film: Mugen Train, 2020 — CANON)

- **Local date:** Taishō, weeks after Campaign 0.1.
- **Protagonist:** Tanjiro, with Flame Hashira Kyojuro Rengoku as co-lead.
- **Antagonist:** Enmu, Lower Rank One (dream/blood demon art); Azaka? No — **Akaza**, Upper Rank Three, as end-boss.
- **Party / main cast:** Zenitsu, Inosuke, Nezuko; Rengoku as the campaign's pillar.
- **Tools & unlocks:** Dream-entry and dream-exit (T1 Veilcraft — the first *mapped* excursion into the Veil's middle layer), Flame Breathing Form Nine shown in full (discipline data), Akaza's Compass Needle (flesh-reading precognition — a combat AI module), the tragedy economy: Rengoku's death converts to the **Oath of the Fallen Pillar** (vow currency, +1 permanent party buff).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks **Veil-walking** as a travel mechanic (dreams as corridors between human minds).
- **Veil-hook:** Rengoku dies standing, fulfilling his vow to protect everyone on the train. That is the first fully-documented **death-oath** — the exact mechanism that later powers the Belmont whip ("alive with the clan's oath"), the Uzumaki sealing formulas, and post-mortem nen. The mugen train is also the first time the Veil's middle layer is charted: dream-space is contiguous, shared, and *mapped* by Enmu like a castle. Muzan's lieutenant builds a pocket world out of forty sleeping minds — the proof of concept for every domain, every Greed Island, every Infinity Castle after.

### Campaign 0.3 — "The Pleasure Quarter Siege" (Season 2: Entertainment District Arc, 2021–22)

- **Local date:** Taishō, Yoshiwara.
- **Protagonist:** Sound Hashira Tengen Uzui and the trio.
- **Antagonist:** Upper Rank Six — Daki and Gyutaro, the shared-body siblings.
- **Party / main cast:** Tengen, his three wives (intelligence network), Nezuko (awakens her advanced demon form), Uzui's retired-corp medic craft.
- **Tools & unlocks:** Sound Breathing + Musical Score combat (rhythm-predictive fighting — T3 discipline), poison pharmacology and antidotes (T2 craft), obi-flesh storage biotech (demon materials: regenerating cloth, flesh pockets — T2 arms), Nezuku's blood-demon ignition (fire that burns demon blood — first seen **sun-fire in a demon's body**, the paradox that the Vessel Cycle keeps circling).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks the **Anti-Venom** and **Infiltration** branches of the corps tree; district tiles as stealth-maps.
- **Veil-hook:** The district sits over a buried shrine; the demons nested there because the Veil runs thin under old sacred ground. Every later age's map of "thin places" — Gresit, the Paris catacombs, Ba Sing Se's sealed gate, Shibuya — inherits this survey.

### Campaign 0.4 — "Village of the Forged Sun" (Season 3: Swordsmith Village Arc, 2023)

- **Local date:** Taishō, the hidden swordsmith village.
- **Protagonist:** Tanjiro; co-leads Mist Hashira Muichiro Tokito and Love Hashira Mitsuri Kanroji.
- **Antagonist:** Upper Rank Five Gyokko and Upper Rank Four Hantengu.
- **Party / main cast:** Hotaru Haganezuka, the ancient training doll **Yoriichi Type Zero**, Kotetsu, Genya Shinazugawa (demon-eating human — flesh-alchemy preview).
- **Tools & unlocks:** **The Mark of the Slayer** awakens (T3 discipline — the body's oath made visible), Bright Red Blade technique (T4 arms), Yoriichi Type Zero automaton (T3 craft: a centuries-old combat doll, proof that pre-modern forge-tech built androids), Muichiro's recovered memory (bloodline data: the sun-line runs through the Tokito line).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks **Slayer Mark resonance** — nearby marked fighters accelerate each other's awakening (mechanic: buff zones).
- **Veil-hook:** Yoriichi Type Zero is built in the image of **Yoriichi Tsugikuni** — the man whose body held the sun without ever seeking godhood (Law 4, Vessel Cycle entry one). The doll survives the Cycle. It sits in a Speaker archive three hundred years later, and a young fire-glyph scholar named Sypha Belnades spars with it. One of its arms is sun-metal. It will eventually be melted down for a whip.

### Campaign 0.5 — "The Pillar Vigil" (Season 4: Hashira Training Arc, 2024)

- **Local date:** Taishō, the Corps compound.
- **Protagonist:** The Hashira collectively; Tanjiro as binding point.
- **Antagonist:** Muzan's probing strikes; the shadow of Upper Rank One Kokushibo (Yoriichi's brother, the moon that failed).
- **Party / main cast:** All nine Hashira; Sanemi, Obanai, Giyu, Shinobu, Mitsuri, Muichiro, Gyomei; Ubuyashiki's final plan.
- **Tools & unlocks:** Total Concentration **Constant** training (T2 discipline mastered), Mark resonance drills, the Ubuyashiki **vow-network** formalized — the corps commander's curse-derived foresight spent to bait Muzan (T4 Veilcraft: a prophet's sacrifice as strategic weapon).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks **corps-wide coordination** (party-swapping mid-battle) and the **Final War** scenario flag.
- **Veil-hook:** Ubuyashiki — a man whose bloodline was cursed by Muzan himself — spends his lifespan as bait. That is the largest single vow in Cycle 0, and the spirit world takes note. In Cycle 2 a dhampir will cite the Belmont-Belnades line as "a family of people who kept paying." The corps' ledger of vows is what the Belmont oath inherits.

### Campaign 0.6 — "The Labyrinth of the First Demon" (Film Trilogy: Infinity Castle, 2025–2029 — CANON)

- **Local date:** Taishō, the night of the final war.
- **Protagonist:** The entire Demon Slayer Corps, all Marks awake.
- **Antagonist:** Muzan Kibutsuji; Upper Rank One Kokushibo (moon-breathing fallen twin), Upper Rank Two Doma, Akaza's last stand, Nakime the biwa-demon whose blood-demon art **is** the castle.
- **Party / main cast:** Tanjiro, the Hashira, Tamayo's final medicine, the Kamado bloodline revelations (Sun Breathing = the Hinokami Kagura, kept in a dance).
- **Tools & unlocks:** Transparent World (T4 discipline — perceive the body's flows), Selfless State, the true Bright Red Blade en masse, Nakime's **living architecture** (T4 Veilcraft: space-folded stronghold responsive to one will), Tamayo's anti-Muzan drug (T4 craft: four-stage poison that ages a demon 9,000 years per hour — the single greatest pharm-tech of the Cycle).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Ends Cycle 0. Grants the age's mastery nodes and closes the **Breathing** branch of the tree forever — nothing in later ages surpasses it as pure internal discipline until Nen re-discovers it internally.
- **Veil-hook (the big one):** Muzan dies, but his **blood-will doesn't**. Demon blood is concentrated negative will — the first cursed energy the world ever made. When the Infinity Castle collapses, that blood-will sinks through the Veil's floor into the lower spectrum's sump, where it pools for a thousand years. Cycle 1's night creatures are it surfacing through alchemy. Cycle 5's tailed beasts are it crystallizing around spirit-vine energy. Cycle 7's cursed energy — the ambient output of human negative emotion, finally teachable — is it finishing its long digestion and becoming a *system*. And the castle itself — a labyrinth folded inside a demon's body that moves at the master's will — is the architectural ancestor of every domain expansion and every sealed battlefield to come. The final war of Cycle 0 is, mechanically, the first draft of Shibuya.

# CYCLE 1 — THE LONG NIGHT (Castlevania)

**Age overview.** Two hundred years after the Corps' final war, breathing scrolls survive only inside the **Speakers** — the slayer survivors' successor order: nomadic, oral-tradition, hiding as entertainers and storytellers (campaign 0.x epilogue: the corps' archivists take the breathing scrolls and vanish into the road). Vampire ecology replaces demon ecology in the lower spectrum: demons were flesh-bound; vampires are *oath-bound*, which is worse. The Church rules surface Europe; magic is heresy; the Belmonts carry the whip.

**Age tech tree:**
- **Discipline:** T1 Speaker glyph-magic (breathing translated to spoken word) → T2 Belmont oath-consecration → T3 forgemaster devil-craft → T4 corridor cartography
- **Arms:** T1 consecrated steel → T2 the Morning Star (Vampire Killer) → T3 alchemic ammunition (explosive rounds, holy water) → T4 the castle-killing rites
- **Craft:** T1 Speaker field-medicine (Lisa's ideal) → T2 night-creature forging (Isaac/Hector) → T3 Carmilla's war-machine (the walking castle) → T4 the Rebis vessel
- **Veilcraft:** T1 hell-forge contracts → T2 Dracula's moving castle → T3 the Infinite Corridor → T4 true resurrection rites

---

### Campaign 1.1 — "The Gresit Heresy" (Season 1, 2017)

- **Local date:** 1476, Wallachia.
- **Protagonist:** Trevor Belmont — last Belmont, drunk, disgraced, and still the best weapon Europe has.
- **Antagonist:** Dracula Vlad Tepes, grieving apocalypse; the Church's corrupt bishops as his unwitting heralds; the Cyclops.
- **Party / main cast:** Sypha Belnades (Speaker magician — the missing link made flesh), Adrian "Alucard" Tepes (dhampir son of Dracula and Lisa).
- **Tools & unlocks:** The **Morning Star** whip (T2 arms: sun-metal chain reforged with the corps' vow-ledger in its alloy — the Oath of the Fallen Pillar hums in it), Speaker fire/ice/lightning glyph set (T1 discipline: the breathing scrolls as *spoken* elements), Belmont combat rites (T2 discipline), the founding of the **Trio Pact** (the series' central vow: hunters and a vampire's son, bound by choice not blood).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks the **Belmont** faction and the glyph-casting class. Whip class: relic-tier weapon that scales with vow-inventory.
- **Veil-hook:** Sypha reads from the Speaker archive — scrolls she can't fully translate, written in a dead corps' notation. The Type Zero doll's arm is already in that archive's vault. She marries Trevor. Every Belmont-Belnades after — **Richter** — carries breathing-derived magic in the blood, which is why Belmonts of this line occasionally do impossible things with the whip's fire. The Belmonts don't know they're the Corps' inheritors. The spirit world knows.

### Campaign 1.2 — "The Forgemaster's War" (Season 2, 2018)

- **Local date:** 1476–1477, Dracula's moving castle and Carmilla's court.
- **Protagonist:** The Trio — Trevor, Sypha, Alucard — plus the defecting forgemaster arc.
- **Antagonist:** Dracula (end-boss); **Carmilla** and her vampire sisterhood as the mid-game usurpers; Isaac and Hector as tragic lieutenants.
- **Party / main cast:** Alucard (the inside man — Dracula's son), Hector (forgemaster wavering), Isaac (forgemaster devout), Carmilla's council, Godbrand.
- **Tools & unlocks:** **Night-creature forging** (T2 Veilcraft/Craft: hell-forge contracts — a human corpse + hell materials + a devil-smith's will = soldier; the industry's moral cost tracked as corruption meter), the moving castle as a siege unit (T2 Veilcraft: a stronghold that is also a creature, answering one will), Carmilla's strategic doctrine (war-economy tech).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks the **Forgemaster** class — dark craft branch: build units from fallen enemies at corruption cost. First appearance of *flesh as raw material*, which Cycle 5 (reanimation jutsu) and Cycle 7 (Mahito's soul-sculpting) will industrialize.
- **Veil-hook:** Dracula's genocide is powered by grief — Lisa's execution. **Vaatu's residue feeds on exactly this.** The being called **Death** (revealed fully in 1.4) attaches to Dracula's despair like a lamprey; every war of this age is his meal. Alucard — half human, half lower-spectrum — is the age's walking treaty, and the first proof since Nezuko that the spectrum can be straddled by *bond* rather than force. Cycle 2's dhampir diplomacy starts here.

### Campaign 1.3 — "The Corridor Pilgrim" (Season 3, 2020)

- **Local date:** 1477–1478, Lindenfeld; Styria.
- **Protagonist:** Three interleaved parties: Trevor & Sypha in Lindenfeld; Alucard at his empty castle; **Saint Germain** in the Infinite Corridor.
- **Antagonist:** Carmilla's empire-in-waiting; the Judge's serial rot beneath wholesome Lindenfeld; Alucard's betrayal by Taka and Sumi (the cost of being a bridge).
- **Party / main cast:** Saint Germain (alchemist, corridor-walker, the age's Veil cartographer), Sala's cult, Taka & Sumi, Carmilla's army.
- **Tools & unlocks:** **Infinite Corridor mapping** (T3 Veilcraft: a nexus of doors to every world, every time, every possible timeline — the age's single greatest tech), alchemical resurrection research (germ of the Rebis), Lindenfeld's priory cult (mass-delusion as Veil-thinning ritual).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks **Corridor travel** — the fast-travel network and the save system. Also unlocks the **Betrayal** event class: bonds broken yield enemy AI with party move-sets (Taka/Sumi fight you with everything Alucard taught).
- **Veil-hook:** Germain walks the Corridor and glimpses, at its center, a great tree and two spirits — **the Tree of Time, Raava and Vaatu.** He doesn't have the words for what he sees (these events belong to Cycle 3's flashback era; the Corridor folds time, remember). He writes *one line* in the corridor-dialect the Speakers will preserve: *"At the heart of all doors, a bond that is not a meal."* That line is quoted in Cycle 2 by Annette's grandmother, and in Cycle 3 by a lion turtle. It's the Vessel Cycle's whole thesis in nine words.

### Campaign 1.4 — "The Rebis Gambit" (Season 4, 2021)

- **Local date:** 1491, Targoviste and the castle's return.
- **Protagonist:** The reunited armies — Trevor & Sypha's insurgency, Alucard's nation-building, Isaac's marching legion of the redeemed.
- **Antagonist:** **Death** — Varney — the true architect, who manufactured Dracula's return to feast forever; the vampire remnants.
- **Party / main cast:** Trevor, Sypha, Alucard, Isaac (the forge turned toward defense), Hector and Lenore (the leash and the leashed), Saint Germain (manipulated toward the Rebis).
- **Tools & unlocks:** **The Rebis ritual** (T4 craft/Veilcraft — forced fusion of opposites into a perfect being; *fails* because it's welded, not bonded; becomes the age's forbidden tech), the true resurrection of Dracula and Lisa (T4 Veilcraft — done right: consent, release, rest; the castle's chaos-heart is finally buried), Isaac's redeemed-forge doctrine.
- **Tech-tree effect:** Ends Cycle 1. Closes the **Forgemaster** branch to players (post-war treaty); opens the **Belmont Diaspora** — the family scatters, carrying the whip-line into Cycle 2.
- **Veil-hook:** Death's plan was the Rebis as a Dracula-vessel — the Vessel Cycle's forced-fusion entry, one cycle before Báthory tries *consuming* a goddess and three cycles before Kenjaku tries merging all of humanity with Tengen. Dracula and Lisa's true resurrection is the other entry: the first time the lower spectrum *releases* someone by choice. The age ends with a lesson the spirit world writes down: bonds work; meals don't. It takes humanity one more false messiah to accept the memo.

# CYCLE 2 — THE VEILED REVOLUTION (Castlevania: Nocturne)

**Age overview.** 1792. Three centuries after the Rebis Gambit, the Belmont-Belnades line has thinned to one hunted orphan in revolutionary France, and the Speakers are a rumor even among magicians. Two new forces enter play: **ancestral communion** (Annette's Vodou line, channeling the enslaved dead of Saint-Domingue) and **revolutionary industry** (the National Guard, the guillotine, mass politics). Vampire aristocracy meets mass democracy — and a vampire messiah tries to become a god.

**Age tech tree:**
- **Discipline:** T1 bloodline resonance (Belmont awakening) → T2 Speaker-survival magic → T3 ancestral communion → T4 god-vessel absorption
- **Arms:** T1 standard hunter kit → T2 the unbound Morning Star → T3 revolutionary mass armaments vs. night creatures
- **Craft:** T1 abbey machinery (Emmanuel's forge) → T2 night-creature industrial line → T3 political mass-mobilization (revolution as tech)
- **Veilcraft:** T1 spirit-world journey (the Duat) → T2 Sekhmet's divided soul → T3 possession as interface → T4 the false apotheosis

### Campaign 2.1 — "The Red Messiah" (Season 1, 2023)

- **Local date:** 1792, Machecoul, Vendée, France.
- **Protagonist:** Richter Belmont — orphan of the whip-line, talented, blocked by trauma.
- **Antagonist:** **Erzsebet Báthory**, the Vampire Messiah, vessel-candidate for Sekhmet; her herald Drolta Tzuentes; Olrox (Aztec vampire noble, sympathetic mirror); the Abbot Emmanuel (night-creature industrialist).
- **Party / main cast:** Annette (descendant of Vodou practitioners — ancestral channeling), Maria Renard (Speaker-raised beast-summoner), Tera (Maria's mother, doomed host), Edouard (singer, first self-aware night creature), Mizrak (ex-abbey knight).
- **Tools & unlocks:** Annette's **ancestral communion** (T3 discipline: direct past-lives power transfer — the Avatar State's core mechanic minus a cosmic partner), Maria's beast/fay familiar contracts (T2 Veilcraft — spirit-summons as legal pacts), the Abbot's forge (night-creature industrial line), the solar eclipse ritual (T4 Veilcraft in progress).
- **Tech-tree effect:** Unlocks the **Channeler** class (ancestral-spirit borrowing: temporary kits from your bloodline's dead) and the **Familiar** system.
- **Veil-hook:** Báthory's apotheosis is the **inverted Avatar State** — same visual grammar (an ancient power flooding a mortal vessel), inverted polarity: *consumption* instead of *bond*. Drolta has spent centuries finding the "perfect vessel," just as the alchemists sought the perfect Rebis. Both entries in the Vessel Cycle fail in the same generation, within a century of each other — and the spirit world draws the conclusion. One Cycle later, Wan does it right. Also note Olrox: an immortal indigenous survivor of colonial vampirism, the age's proof that the vampire problem is political, not just spiritual — a nuance Cycle 7's curse-users will rediscover.

### Campaign 2.2 — "The Blood of the Sun" (Season 2, 2025 — finale)

- **Local date:** 1792–1793, Machecoul to Paris.
- **Protagonist:** Richter, Annette; Juste Belmont (Richter's grandfather, the line's ember) and Alucard (returning legend) as late-campaign pillars.
- **Antagonist:** Báthory-as-Sekhmet (false goddess), Drolta (true believer who eats her messiah when found unworthy — the meal metaphor completing itself), Olrox's gambit line, Emmanuel's dragon.
- **Party / main cast:** Alucard, Juste, Maria (loses mother, nearly turns — dark-magic temptation arc), Mizrak (mortally wounded, turned by Olrox — the cycle's mercy), Edouard and the captain's night-creature defector corps.
- **Tools & unlocks:** **The unbound Morning Star** (T2 arms full power — Richter's storm-of-fire moment, the whip's oath-ledger finally reading him as worthy), **spirit-world journeying** (T1 Veilcraft perfected: Annette walks the Duat, meets her mother and Cecile, petitions Ogun — the first fully-mapped solo spirit quest in the timeline), possession-as-interface (Sekhmet rides Annette's body — a *consensual* possession, the bond made visible against Báthory's theft), revolutionary mass combat (Paris under blood-red sky; humans, rebels, and redeemed night creatures on one field).

# CYCLE 2.5 — THE SEVERANCE (Gap Ruleset, ~1700s–pre-Wan)

**No source material — this is a ruleset, not a campaign.** Between Nocturne and the Avatar era, the spirit world withdraws by treaty of disgust:

- **Magic is persecuted, not practiced.** Speakers burned as heretics; Belmont records destroyed; the whip becomes folklore. Playable rule: **discipline checks auto-fail** unless the character carries a bloodline feat.
- **Bloodlines go dormant.** The Kamado sun-line, the Belnades glyph-line, Annette's channeler line — all recessive, waiting for an awakening trigger.
- **Craft advances without Veilcraft.** Steam, iron, gunpowder, the industrial revolution — the world's first tech tree climbing *without* magic. This is deliberate: the spirits want to see if humanity can exist without them.
- **Thin places persist.** The old survey sites (Gresit, Machecoul's abbey, the Paris catacombs, later Ba Sing Se's foundations) stay on the map as dormant nodes.
- **The lion turtles stay.** They alone don't withdraw — they are the spirits' control group, carrying the bending seeds until the withdrawal ends.

**Veil-hook:** This Cycle's whole point is the question Wan answers and Korra re-opens: *can the two worlds live apart?* Every age after is an experiment with different answers — Wan's separation, Korra's integration, Naruto-era dilution, Jujutsu-era forced cohabitation.

# PART V — CROSS-AGE GAMEPLAY RULES

1. **The Vow Economy is the meta-currency across all Cycles.** Every campaign's sacrifices feed a persistent player-wide ledger: Rengoku's stand, Ubuyashiki's bait, Hiruzen's seal, Chiyo's trade, Netero's Rose, Riko's failed escort, Nanami's overtime. Spending vow-points unlocks cross-cycle relic access (the whip recognizes a holder who has paid).

2. **Relics persist across Cycles.** The Type Zero doll, the whip, the whip's sun-metal, the corridor-dialect grammar, Wan Shi Tong's shelves. A player who knows where a relic slept in Cycle N can retrieve it in Cycle N+2 — at the cost of disturbing whatever has been sleeping next to it.

3. **The Spectrum is the difficulty slider.** Lower-spectrum zones (hell-forges, Shibuya night, the Infinity Castle) buff corruption builds and debuff bonds; upper-spectrum zones (spirit oases, the Duat, portals) do the reverse. Mid-spectrum zones (Heavens Arena, Ba Sing Se's undercity, the Foggy Swamp) are wildcards where both build types pay hidden costs.

4. **Withdrawals are soft wipes.** When a Cycle ends, disciplines don't vanish — they go *latent*. A character built in Cycle 5's chakra tree playing in Cycle 7 maps to cursed-energy equivalents at 60% efficiency, and vice versa. Only breathing and nen (the two fully internal disciplines) transfer at full power — the timeline's argument that what you truly learn, you keep.

5. **The Culling Game is the current endgame raid.** It is the convergence of every system: vow economy (binding rules), relic persistence (Prison Realm), flesh-craft (Kenjaku), pocket worlds (colony domains), the Vessel Cycle (the Merger), and the exam question the whole timeline has been studying since Muzan took his medicine: *what does a perfect vessel look like?*

**Closing line for the game box:** *Before the Avatar, there were the Belmonts. The difference is the Belmonts fought chaos forever — the Avatar ended the forever. You're playing in the bill that came due.*
