Army size: small fights stay small, big hosts shrink together

CK3 counts soldiers per regiment. Three Kingdoms fields at most 21 unit cards per army: three generals, each leading six units. At the unit size in your G1 runs a militia card holds 80 men and a general's card 21, so one army tops out at 1,503 men.

Measure
Take fighting men F per side from the CK3 battle record at export. Kasr al-Kabir: 340.10 attacking, 421.48 defending (of 571 and 528 at the start).
Shrink
s = min(1, 1503 ÷ F_att, 1503 ÷ F_def). Both sides use the same s, so the odds survive. Below 1,503 men a side, nothing shrinks.
Fill
Target T = round(F × s). Use the fewest generals whose retinues (six units each) can hold the side: commander first, then the highest-prowess knights, three at most. Units n = ceil((T − 21g) ÷ 80). Trim the surplus (under 80 men) at deployment with unit:kill_number_of_men. The game's own battle library calls it (lib_battle_script_unit.lua:1334); we haven't tested it.
Return
CK3 dead per side = round(F × lost ÷ started). The casualty rate crosses, not the head count. started is the count logged at battle start, not the planned one, because unit size follows the player's graphics setting. Split the total across regiments by current strength with largest-remainder rounding so the side's total is exact (G2 chunk attribution).
Resolve
Proposal for Defect 6. The loser's surviving 3K men were routing. Record them as CK3 soft casualties so the loser has no fighting men left and CK3 ends the battle for the 3K winner. Test this on the G2 fixture before building on it.

Crusader Kings III: one button, then a paused game

CW1 already proved the CK3 half. Its Swords button in the battle window runs eight scripted steps that print the battle into the debug log, then opens the pause menu and saves. CW2 keeps that pattern and changes the text. The player's side sits on the left, as in CK3; at Kasr al-Kabir the player defended.

  • Reused from CW1: the button, the first-casualty gate, the scripted steps that write to the debug log (participants, armies, characters, battle, terrain, modifiers) and the pause-and-save step.
  • New for CW2: its own marker keyword and save names, and the tooltip text. The scale preview lives in the launcher, not in CK3.
  • CW1 and CW2 can't share a playset. Both replace gui/window_combat.gui, so disable CW1 when testing CW2.

Fixes for the launcher spec

docs/design/FRONTEND_DESIGN.md stays the launcher plan. These five changes bring it in line with the evidence above.

  • Small fights get inflated. slots_for turns Kasr al-Kabir (340 v 421) into 15 v 18 slots: 1,200 v 1,440 men in 3K. This page's rule stays at 1 : 1 until a side passes 1,503 men, giving 5 v 6 cards.
  • Generals count toward the cap. MAX_SLOTS = 18 leaves them out. 3K caps an army at 21 cards with six units per general, so the second and third generals join only when a side needs more than 6 or 12 units.
  • The winner can be filled in now. BattleResult.winner comes from the logged result (routing_state, or engine_callback if the engine ever sends it), mapped through the manifest's side map. The player defended at Kasr al-Kabir.
  • Same loss rule, different denominator. ck3_losses already scales by casualty rate. Take tk_men from the logged start count, since unit size follows the graphics setting.
  • CK3 can stay open. Writing to a new _AFTER file doesn't touch the open save, so the player can stay paused and use Load Game. Keep the “CK3 must be closed” block only for writes that overwrite an existing save, and confirm this during the G2 reload proof.
